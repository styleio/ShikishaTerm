## Check on your AI from your phone

Scan a QR code and you can see what every tab is doing and send instructions — from the
train, from a café, from bed. Over [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) only your own devices
can reach it, encrypted, from anywhere.

## Why it exists

Running one terminal AI is easy. Running **four** is not.

You end up with a window per agent, no idea which one is waiting for you, and a lot of
copy-pasting between them. SHIKISHA-TERM is a terminal that knows the difference between
working, finished and waiting for a human — and can move work between agents on its own.

It talks to whatever runs in a terminal, so it is not tied to one vendor: Claude Code,
Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Ollama, Aider, or a plain shell over SSH.

## Get started

1. Download the zip from [Releases](https://github.com/styleio/ShikishaTerm/releases/latest)
   and unzip it anywhere — a USB stick and a synced folder both work
2. Run `SHIKISHA-TERM.exe`
3. Press `[e]` to open the settings screen in your browser and pick which AI runs where

Nothing is written outside that folder.

:::note[Where it fits]
**Windows only** today — it is built on ConPTY. It **drives CLIs rather than replacing
them**: your subscriptions, logins and settings stay exactly as they are. And it is a
terminal, not an IDE.
:::
