# Logos in the works-with band

The service logos are the monochrome icons from `@lobehub/icons-static-svg`
(MIT), with their size, style and title attributes taken off so the page sets
them. Each mark is the trademark of its owner and is shown only to say that
the app connects to that service.

`terminal.svg` and `choice.svg` are drawn here for the entries that have no mark
of their own (a CLI without a logo, a shell over SSH, a decision model).
